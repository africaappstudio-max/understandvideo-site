Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

On your computer

  • Understand This Video For Me AI Plugin processes only video files already on your computer. ffmpeg extracts the key frames and audio, and faster-whisper turns speech into a transcript, all on your computer.
  • Your original video file is never uploaded by us. We never receive, see or store your videos, frames or transcripts.
  • The plugin itself sends nothing to any AI provider. It makes no requests to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI or any other AI service.

Your chosen AI assistant

  • The plugin hands its results (key frames, one overview image, the transcript and a timeline) to the AI assistant you choose and run, such as Claude Desktop, Cursor or Grok Bot.
  • If that assistant uses a cloud AI model, for example from Anthropic, OpenAI or xAI, your assistant sends that content to the provider. It is then processed under that provider's terms and privacy policy, not ours. If your assistant uses a model on your own computer, it stays on your computer.

The only network connections the plugin makes

  • No video downloads: the plugin only processes video files already on your computer. It does not download videos from websites. If you want a video from the internet processed, download or export it yourself first, then give the plugin the file path.
  • Speech model: the first time it transcribes, faster-whisper downloads the free Whisper speech model once (from Hugging Face). No audio or video is sent.
  • License: activation and an occasional re-check send only your license key, a device ID and your device name to Freemius, our licensing provider. No video content is sent.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Purchases and your data

  • When you buy, Freemius processes your name, email and payment details to complete the purchase and send your license. We use your email only for license and support matters.
  • We do not sell your data. For questions or data requests (access, correction, deletion), email video@shipmonthly.com.
