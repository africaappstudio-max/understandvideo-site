How it works.

Three steps. The video is processed on your computer.

1

Put your video in the folder

The installer adds a folder called Understand This Video to your Desktop. Put any video in it: a tutorial, a screen recording, an ad, a webinar.

2

How to use it.

No file paths. Put a video in a folder, then ask.

  1. 1

    Put your video in the folder

    The installer put a folder called Understand This Video on your Desktop. Move or save your video into it. (It's not there? Make a new folder on your Desktop with exactly that name.)

  2. 2

    Close and reopen your AI app once

    Mac: press Cmd+Q in Claude, then open it again. Windows: right-click Claude's icon near the clock, choose Quit, then open it again. You only do this once, right after installing.

  3. 3

    Ask your AI

    Start a new chat and type: Understand my latest video

    Or use the file name: Understand My Video.mp4. Capital letters and the ending (.mp4) don't matter, and part of the name is enough. If Claude asks, click Allow. A Mac may also ask if Claude can access your Desktop: click OK.

Good to know. It works on video files saved on your computer, not links, so download a video first. Attaching a video to the chat doesn't use the plugin; put it in the folder instead. The folder is just a handy spot: the plugin also finds videos in your Downloads, Movies and Videos folders by name.

Optional: use a video from another folder

Mac: click the video once, hold the Option key, right-click it and choose Copy "…" as Pathname. In Claude type Understand this video for me: and paste.

Windows: hold Shift, right-click the video and choose Copy as path (on Windows 11 a normal right-click works too). In Claude type Understand this video for me: and paste.

Questions.

What is Understand This Video Plugin?

It's the AI plugin that watches and listens to videos for you. It gives Claude Desktop, Cursor or Grok Bot the key frames and a timestamped transcript of any video on your computer, so your AI can explain it, summarize it and answer questions about it.

Do I have to type a file path?

No. Put your video in the Understand This Video folder on your Desktop and ask: Understand my latest video, or use the file name. Attaching a video to the chat does not use the plugin, so use the folder.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Frames and transcripts are created locally on your computer. The plugin never uploads your video and never contacts any AI provider. We never receive your video or anything made from it.

The plugin hands the frames and transcript to the AI assistant you choose (Claude Desktop, Cursor, Grok Bot and others). If that assistant uses a cloud AI model, for example from Anthropic, OpenAI or xAI, your assistant sends that content to the provider, and it is handled under that provider's terms and privacy policy.
